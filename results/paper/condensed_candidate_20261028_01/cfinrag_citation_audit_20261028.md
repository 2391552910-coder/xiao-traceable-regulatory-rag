## Scope checked

The publisher record describes CFinRAG as a Chinese financial event knowledge-graph RAG framework with semantic-aware chunking, LLM-based triplet extraction, query reconstruction, and dual-level retrieval. The article reports a curated dataset of 1,200 Chinese financial research reports and 3,600 QA pairs, and evaluates single-hop, multi-hop, and open-ended tasks. The manuscript now cites the article as a related financial-domain system and distinguishes its graph-based event-QA objective from the current study's fixed-corpus comparison of annual-report/regulation source separation against merged retrieval.

The distinction is an interpretation of the two studies' stated methods and tasks; it does not claim that CFinRAG lacks every source-role feature or that the present work is globally novel.
