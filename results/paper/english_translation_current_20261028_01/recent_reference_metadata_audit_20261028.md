## Scope

This audit checks bibliographic metadata for references [5], [6], [11], [15], [17], [18], [28], [29], and [30] in the English working translation. Publisher pages, the ACL Anthology, and the author-affiliated CityUHK research portal were used to confirm titles, author order, publication venue/year, volume or proceedings/pages, article numbers, and DOI. This is a metadata check only; it does not validate every claim attributed to these papers or complete a reference-by-reference audit of [1]–[31].
